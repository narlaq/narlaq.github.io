How it works

One washer, one proper

Each accomplishment earns one anodized M10 washer in its vibe's color.

The stamper

A rider with a stack watches it happen or checks the proof, then stamps the date and their initials into the washer. No stamp, no proper.

The Sprout comes first

Your first proper comes from whoever brought you to your first lot day or group ride.

Capstones

One accomplishment in five of the vibes earns a bigger 30 mm washer that stands out in the stack.

Stunts stay on the lot

Stunt propers are only witnessed at lot days or closed venues.

Earned, never traded

Blank washers can be shared. Stamped ones stay with the rider who earned them.
